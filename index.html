Explore the High Ranges from Mundakayam

മുണ്ടക്കയത്തു നിന്നുള്ള വിനോദസഞ്ചാര കേന്ദ്രങ്ങൾ

Positioned at the base of the Western Ghats, Hotel Vazhiyoram is the natural springboard to Kerala's most scenic hill destinations.

Vagamon Pine Forests & Meadows

Approx. 30 km • 45 min drive

Famous pine hills, lush green rolling meadows, Kurisumala ashram, and cool mist.

Thekkady & Periyar Sanctuary

Approx. 65 km • 1.5 hrs drive

Periyar National Park boating, wild elephant reserves, and spice plantation tours in Kumily.

Peerumedu & Kuttikkanam

Approx. 28 km • 40 min drive

Historic tea plantations, Ashley Bungalow, summer palaces, and mountain vantage points.

Erumely Sabarimala Gateway

Approx. 35 km • 45 min drive

Historic Petta Thullal, Vavar Mosque, and Dharma Sastha Temple on the traditional pilgrimage route.

St. Alphonsa Shrine, Bharananganam

Approx. 40 km • 50 min drive

Prominent Christian pilgrimage destination venerating Saint Alphonsa near Pala.

Kottayam Railway Station

Approx. 52 km • 1.2 hrs drive

Main railhead connecting to Kochi, Trivandrum, Chennai, and national railway networks.
